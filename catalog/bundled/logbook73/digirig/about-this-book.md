# About this book

This guide to the Digirig Mobile was written by Tyler Allen for LogBook73, an amateur radio logbook, and is included with Outpost under LogBook73's MIT licence.

It is not the manufacturer's manual, and it is not legal advice. What you may transmit, on which frequencies and at what power is set by your own licence and the rules of the country you are in.

## How it was reviewed

LogBook73 last reviewed this guide's menu and specification claims on 9 September 2026.

The official Digirig product pages, getting-started guide, serial-configuration guide and digital-modes troubleshooting guide were the factual baseline; community practice beyond the officially supported systems is labelled as such.

| Reviewed against | |
| --- | --- |
| Models | Digirig Mobile (hardware revision 1.11, connector-compatible back to 1.6) with the Digirig Lite covered as an explicit comparison, not a subject. |
| Regions | Interface behaviour is global; band plans, data sub-bands and identification rules remain matters for the operator’s national licence. |
| Manuals | digirig.net product pages, Getting Started with Digirig Mobile, the serial-configuration selection guide and the Troubleshooting Digital Modes guide. |
| Firmware | No device firmware is user-updatable; behaviour differences track hardware revision and serial configuration, both stated per the official pages rather than assumed. |

The sources the review started from:

- [Getting Started with Digirig Mobile](https://digirig.net/getting-started-with-digirig-mobile/), the manufacturer's manual
- [Digirig Mobile product page (hardware revision and internals)](https://digirig.net/product/digirig-mobile/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Selecting Digirig Mobile serial configuration](https://digirig.net/selecting-digirig-configuration/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Digirig Troubleshooting Digital Modes guide](https://digirig.net/troubleshooting-digital-modes/), the manufacturer's support pages

Outpost has not checked the guide against those manuals again. Firmware changes, and a radio sold under the same name can differ from one market to another: where the guide and your radio disagree, believe the radio and its own manual.

## In Outpost

This copy was made for Outpost from LogBook73's files by a script, so that it can be read with no connection. The chapters are as their author wrote them.

The guide often points to a chapter of the Radio Engineering Handbook for the theory behind a setting. Those links open the chapter when the handbook is in your library; when it is not, they say so and lead to it in Explore.

Not carried over from LogBook73:

- **Self-check quizzes and reading progress.** LogBook73 follows which chapters you have read and offers questions at the end of a part. Outpost does neither.
- **Pop-up definitions.** In LogBook73 a term can be pressed for its definition. Here the handbook's glossary chapter holds most of the same definitions.
- **LogBook73's own screens.** Where a chapter mentions LogBook73's map, logbook, band tools or practice pages, the words are kept and the link is not: there is nothing in Outpost for it to open.
- **Pictures.** Eleven handbook chapters have an illustration or two. The pictures are not included; the description and caption their author wrote for each one are shown in its place. Every diagram and signal figure that is drawn from the text is here.
