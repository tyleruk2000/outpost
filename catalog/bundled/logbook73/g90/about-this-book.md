# About this book

This guide to the Xiegu G90 was written by Tyler Allen for LogBook73, an amateur radio logbook, and is included with Outpost under LogBook73's MIT licence.

It is not the manufacturer's manual, and it is not legal advice. What you may transmit, on which frequencies and at what power is set by your own licence and the rules of the country you are in.

## How it was reviewed

LogBook73 last reviewed this guide's menu and specification claims on 28 August 2026.

The Xiegu operation manual, manufacturer-hosted control reference, data-mode instructions and current firmware notes were reconciled item by item; older-menu and firmware-dependent behaviour is labelled rather than silently mixed into the current controls.

| Reviewed against | |
| --- | --- |
| Models | Xiegu G90 only; the G90S shares an official update tutorial but is not assumed to have an identical control or regional configuration. |
| Regions | Radio concepts are global; enabled transmit edges, mode permissions and legal power limits vary by market, licence and jurisdiction. |
| Manuals | Xiegu G90 Operation Manual, manufacturer-hosted G90 control reference, firmware upgrade tutorial and G90/X5105 data communication guide. |
| Firmware | Matched APP (display/head) and BASE (main/body) firmware V1.81, released 25 August 2025, is the current baseline reviewed; both installed versions must be identified before updating. |

The sources the review started from:

- [Xiegu G90 operation manual](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_downloads/g90-operations-manual-v104/), the manufacturer's manual
- [Xiegu G90 manuals, firmware and utilities](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_categories/g90/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu G90 matched APP and BASE firmware V1.81 release](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_downloads/xiegu-g90-firmware-v1-81-25-08-25/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu G90 firmware upgrade tutorial](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_downloads/g90-firmware-upgrade-tutorial/), the manufacturer's support pages

Outpost has not checked the guide against those manuals again. Firmware changes, and a radio sold under the same name can differ from one market to another: where the guide and your radio disagree, believe the radio and its own manual.

## In Outpost

This copy was made for Outpost from LogBook73's files by a script, so that it can be read with no connection. The chapters are as their author wrote them.

The guide often points to a chapter of the Radio Engineering Handbook for the theory behind a setting. Those links open the chapter when the handbook is in your library; when it is not, they say so and lead to it in Explore.

Not carried over from LogBook73:

- **Self-check quizzes and reading progress.** LogBook73 follows which chapters you have read and offers questions at the end of a part. Outpost does neither.
- **Pop-up definitions.** In LogBook73 a term can be pressed for its definition. Here the handbook's glossary chapter holds most of the same definitions.
- **LogBook73's own screens.** Where a chapter mentions LogBook73's map, logbook, band tools or practice pages, the words are kept and the link is not: there is nothing in Outpost for it to open.
- **Pictures.** Twelve handbook chapters have an illustration. The pictures are not included; the description and caption their author wrote for each one are shown in its place. Every diagram and signal figure that is drawn from the text is here.
