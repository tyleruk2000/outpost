# About this book

This guide to the (tr)uSDX was written by Tyler Allen for LogBook73, an amateur radio logbook, and is included with Outpost under LogBook73's MIT licence.

It is not the manufacturer's manual, and it is not legal advice. What you may transmit, on which frequencies and at what power is set by your own licence and the rules of the country you are in.

## How it was reviewed

LogBook73 last reviewed this guide's menu and specification claims on 9 September 2026.

The official DL2MAN manual, assembly, bootloader and firmware pages were reconciled with the published uSDX firmware source and official forum release notes; the manual page’s own accuracy disclaimer is honoured by labelling firmware-version differences explicitly throughout.

| Reviewed against | |
| --- | --- |
| Models | (tr)uSDX by DL2MAN and PE1NNZ in its three published filter-board builds (Lo, Hi and Classic bands); unlicensed clones may differ in hardware and cannot use official firmware. |
| Regions | Radio concepts are global; band permissions — 60 m especially — and power limits vary by licence and jurisdiction, and the fitted filter board never overrides them. |
| Manuals | Official DL2MAN (tr)uSDX manual, assembly, bootloader and firmware pages, read together with the open uSDX firmware source that documents the menu system’s semantics. |
| Firmware | The documented 2.00-series baseline, with the 2.00t CAT-baud change, 2.00u audio-over-USB and the 2.00v–x beta menu churn labelled by version; menus on any given build may differ, and the radio’s own screen is treated as final. |

The sources the review started from:

- [Official (tr)uSDX manual page](https://dl2man.de/4-trusdx-manual/), the manufacturer's manual
- [(tr)uSDX firmware download and update page](https://dl2man.de/3b-trusdx-firmware/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [(tr)uSDX assembly and filter-board variants page](https://dl2man.de/2-trusdx-assembly/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [(tr)uSDX bootloader installation page](https://dl2man.de/3a-trusdx-bootloader/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [uSDX firmware source (menu-system lineage)](https://github.com/threeme3/usdx), the firmware project

Outpost has not checked the guide against those manuals again. Firmware changes, and a radio sold under the same name can differ from one market to another: where the guide and your radio disagree, believe the radio and its own manual.

## In Outpost

This copy was made for Outpost from LogBook73's files by a script, so that it can be read with no connection. The chapters are as their author wrote them.

The guide often points to a chapter of the Radio Engineering Handbook for the theory behind a setting. Those links open the chapter when the handbook is in your library; when it is not, they say so and lead to it in Explore.

Not carried over from LogBook73:

- **Self-check quizzes and reading progress.** LogBook73 follows which chapters you have read and offers questions at the end of a part. Outpost does neither.
- **Pop-up definitions.** In LogBook73 a term can be pressed for its definition. Here the handbook's glossary chapter holds most of the same definitions.
- **LogBook73's own screens.** Where a chapter mentions LogBook73's map, logbook, band tools or practice pages, the words are kept and the link is not: there is nothing in Outpost for it to open.
- **Pictures.** Twelve handbook chapters have an illustration. The pictures are not included; the description and caption their author wrote for each one are shown in its place. Every diagram and signal figure that is drawn from the text is here.
