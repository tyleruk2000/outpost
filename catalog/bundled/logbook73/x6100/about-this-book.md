# About this book

This guide to the Xiegu X6100 was written by Tyler Allen for LogBook73, an amateur radio logbook, and is included with Outpost under LogBook73's MIT licence.

It is not the manufacturer's manual, and it is not legal advice. What you may transmit, on which frequencies and at what power is set by your own licence and the rules of the country you are in.

## How it was reviewed

LogBook73 last reviewed this guide's menu and specification claims on 28 August 2026.

The Xiegu EU operations manual, current manufacturer V1.6 manual, official FT8 and update tutorials, and published APP V1.2.0 release notes were reconciled with the guide; manual/firmware differences and community behaviour remain explicitly labelled.

| Reviewed against | |
| --- | --- |
| Models | Xiegu X6100; community SD-card environments are treated separately from stock firmware. |
| Regions | Radio concepts are global; enabled transmit edges and legal privileges vary by market and jurisdiction. |
| Manuals | Xiegu EU X6100 Operations Manual, current manufacturer V1.6 manual, official PC connection / FT8 tutorial, firmware update tutorial and manufacturer release notes. |
| Firmware | Stock APP V1.2.0 (13 December 2025) with BASE V1.1.9 (28 November 2025) is the latest release baseline reviewed; installed versions must still be checked before updating. |

The sources the review started from:

- [Xiegu X6100 operations manual](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_downloads/xiegu-x6100-operations-manual/), the manufacturer's manual
- [Current manufacturer X6100 V1.6 manual](https://www.cqxiegu.com/filedownload/2988637), the manufacturer's manual
- [Xiegu X6100 manuals, firmware and utilities](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_categories/x6100/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu X6100 APP V1.2.0 firmware release notes](https://xiegu.eu/2025/12/22/xiegu-x6100-firmware-update-app-v1-2-0-dec-13-2025/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu X6100 PC connection and FT8 tutorial](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_downloads/xiegu-x6100-pc-connection-ft8-tutorial/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [OpenX6100 x6100_gui community firmware (R1CBU line)](https://github.com/OpenX6100/x6100_gui), the firmware project
- [OpenX6X00 community firmware by RF.Guru](https://github.com/Guru-RF/OpenX6X00), the firmware project

Outpost has not checked the guide against those manuals again. Firmware changes, and a radio sold under the same name can differ from one market to another: where the guide and your radio disagree, believe the radio and its own manual.

## In Outpost

This copy was made for Outpost from LogBook73's files by a script, so that it can be read with no connection. The chapters are as their author wrote them.

The guide often points to a chapter of the Radio Engineering Handbook for the theory behind a setting. Those links open the chapter when the handbook is in your library; when it is not, they say so and lead to it in Explore.

Not carried over from LogBook73:

- **Self-check quizzes and reading progress.** LogBook73 follows which chapters you have read and offers questions at the end of a part. Outpost does neither.
- **Pop-up definitions.** In LogBook73 a term can be pressed for its definition. Here the handbook's glossary chapter holds most of the same definitions.
- **LogBook73's own screens.** Where a chapter mentions LogBook73's map, logbook, band tools or practice pages, the words are kept and the link is not: there is nothing in Outpost for it to open.
- **Pictures.** Twelve handbook chapters have an illustration. The pictures are not included; the description and caption their author wrote for each one are shown in its place. Every diagram and signal figure that is drawn from the text is here.
