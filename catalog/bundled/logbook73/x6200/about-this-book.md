# About this book

This guide to the Xiegu X6200 was written by Tyler Allen for LogBook73, an amateur radio logbook, and is included with Outpost under LogBook73's MIT licence.

It is not the manufacturer's manual, and it is not legal advice. What you may transmit, on which frequencies and at what power is set by your own licence and the rules of the country you are in.

## How it was reviewed

LogBook73 last reviewed this guide's menu and specification claims on 28 August 2026.

Xiegu EU’s public English Rev01 manual and firmware tutorial were reconciled with the manufacturer-written Rev05 mirror, current manufacturer V06 manual, and Xiegu EU V1.0.6/V1.0.7 release notes. The English menu baseline is EU 1.0.7; newer manufacturer/Radioddity release channels are disclosed without mixing unverified 1.0.8 behaviour or recommending cross-channel flashing.

| Reviewed against | |
| --- | --- |
| Models | Stock Xiegu X6200 only; X6100 controls are not carried across by assumption, and WR12 add-on behaviour is identified separately. |
| Regions | Radio concepts are global; enabled transmit edges, market configuration, legal modes and licence privileges vary by jurisdiction. |
| Manuals | Xiegu EU English User Manual Rev01, official brochure and update tutorial, manufacturer-written Rev05 English mirror, and current manufacturer V06 Chinese manual for documented deltas. |
| Firmware | Xiegu EU V1.0.7 (12 October 2025) is the fully documented English baseline. The manufacturer posted a newer 2026 image and Radioddity labels V1.0.8; their behaviour is not silently merged without authoritative English change notes. |

The sources the review started from:

- [Xiegu X6200 English user manual V01](https://xiegu.eu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2024/07/X6200-USER-MANUAL-V01-PileupDX.com.pdf), the manufacturer's manual
- [Xiegu X6200 manuals and firmware releases](https://xiegu.eu/sdm_categories/x6200/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu EU X6200 firmware release notes](https://xiegu.eu/downloads/), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu X6200 firmware upgrade tutorial](https://xiegu.eu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2024/07/X6200FirmwareUpgradeTutorialWS-JSZL-2407-01ENRevised.pdf), the manufacturer's support pages
- [Xiegu manufacturer current manual downloads](https://www.cqxiegu.com/zlxz), the manufacturer's support pages

Outpost has not checked the guide against those manuals again. Firmware changes, and a radio sold under the same name can differ from one market to another: where the guide and your radio disagree, believe the radio and its own manual.

## In Outpost

This copy was made for Outpost from LogBook73's files by a script, so that it can be read with no connection. The chapters are as their author wrote them.

The guide often points to a chapter of the Radio Engineering Handbook for the theory behind a setting. Those links open the chapter when the handbook is in your library; when it is not, they say so and lead to it in Explore.

Not carried over from LogBook73:

- **Self-check quizzes and reading progress.** LogBook73 follows which chapters you have read and offers questions at the end of a part. Outpost does neither.
- **Pop-up definitions.** In LogBook73 a term can be pressed for its definition. Here the handbook's glossary chapter holds most of the same definitions.
- **LogBook73's own screens.** Where a chapter mentions LogBook73's map, logbook, band tools or practice pages, the words are kept and the link is not: there is nothing in Outpost for it to open.
- **Pictures.** Twelve handbook chapters have an illustration. The pictures are not included; the description and caption their author wrote for each one are shown in its place. Every diagram and signal figure that is drawn from the text is here.
