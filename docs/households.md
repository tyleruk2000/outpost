# Households and people

**Preparedness → Households** says who and where Outpost keeps things for. Most
Outposts have one household, called **Home** until you rename it, and everything works
as it always has. Add another when you keep things for more than one place: your own
home and the family's, a home and a second place with mostly the same people, a caravan
or a group on the move.

## Getting ready from nothing

The **Preparedness** page starts with **Getting ready**: five steps in the order each
needs the one before. Who lives here, where home is, supplies, an emergency plan and the
household book. Each is ticked once Outpost holds it, the first not yet done is marked
**Next**, and each goes straight to where it is done.

## What a household is

A household has a name, a home (or, if it moves, where it is now), a country, and the
people and pets in it. Each household has its own:

- supplies, the shopping list and the days wanted;
- emergency plans, the log of events, routine checks and the offline drill;
- medical sheets for nobody in particular, the keeping in touch plan, grab cards and the
  household book;
- the garden's record of sowings.

A power plan is for every household unless you choose one under **For** on the plan (the
caravan's leisure battery, say). Saved places, contacts, notes and the library are shared
by every household.

A person's medical sheet belongs to the person, so it is the same in every household
they are in.

## Fixed and portable

- **Fixed:** a home that stays put. Its home is a saved place: choose one on the
  household's page, or press **Mark home on the map** there. Type coordinates, a grid
  reference or a locator (or, once place names are added on Maps, a town or postcode)
  in **Find the place**, or press the map, which opens on the household's country (else
  your device's region). Or use this device's location. That saves the place on the map with the
  home icon and makes it the household's home in one go.
- **Portable:** a caravan, a car or a hiking group. Its home is wherever you last said
  it is. Press **We're here now** (on Households, the household's page, or Home) and
  choose this device's location, a point on the map, or a saved place. The browser asks
  before it shares the device's location, and only on a page opened over HTTPS or on the
  device itself.

A portable household's place has an age: screens that work from home say "as of 3 hours
ago". Nothing is fetched because it moved. A forecast, tide table or pass list for the
new place is fetched only when you ask, and until then the screen says its figures are
for the earlier place. The household's page lists where it has been, newest first (the
last 50). **Where it is kept** (a storage yard, say) is shown but never taken as where
it is.

**Country** is worked out from home or where it is now, from the countries' outlines
(accurate to about a kilometre). Outpost says which country it took when you mark home,
and the household's page says when home is within a few kilometres of another country,
so you can check. Choose another there if the one worked out is wrong. It decides the
emergency number, weather warnings, the energy table, frequencies and licences.

## People in several households

Someone can be in more than one household: you at home and in the caravan, a parent
who lives with you half the week. Their needs (age, energy and water a day) are the same
in each, and count towards each household's supplies.

On a household's page:

- **Add a person** or **Add a pet** adds someone new, with the same figures as before.
- **Add someone already here** adds someone from another household. Under their name it
  says where else they are ("Also in Home").
- The bin button takes someone out of that household. It never deletes them: they stay
  on the People list.

**Save people** saves the list. A new household can start with the people of another:
choose them under **Start with the people of**.

The **People** list on Households shows everyone and which households each is in.
Deleting someone there takes them out of every household. Their medical sheets are kept,
linked to nobody, and the screen says so before anything goes.

## The current household

One household is current, for the whole Outpost: every screen and every device shows its
supplies, plans and papers, and home-based screens work from its home. Change it with
**Make current** on Households, or with **Household** in the sidebar (on a phone, at the
top of **More**). Changing it changes it for everyone using this Outpost, and open
screens on other devices change with it.

With two or more households, each household's screen says which one it is for under its
title ("For Caravan · Change"). The map shows every household's home, labelled.

## Deleting a household

**Delete** on a household's page first says what goes with it: its supplies, plans, log,
checks and papers. Nobody is deleted from the People list; anyone left in no household
stays on it, with their medical sheets. Outpost always keeps one household, so the last
one cannot be deleted; rename it instead.

## Taking it with you

- **Export everything** gives each household a folder of its own under Supplies,
  Emergency plans, and Medical sheets and keeping in touch, and writes the households, the People list
  and the household book's answers under Households and people.
- **A portable drive** carries every household, with its people, supplies, plans and
  papers, and the drive screen names them.
- **Erase everything** removes every household and person; the next use starts again
  with one household called Home.
