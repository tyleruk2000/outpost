# Radio quick start

For anyone in the household who did not set Outpost up. Everything here works with no
internet. Listening needs no licence; transmitting does, except on licence-free channels.

## Find something to listen to

1. Open **Radio → Frequencies to listen to**.
2. Filter by what you want to hear, your country, and **a set like mine**.
3. Tune to the frequency shown (kHz below 30 MHz, MHz above). Press the station's name for
   what you will hear, when, and the mode to set.

**Make a PDF** puts the filtered list on paper.

## The frequency sheets

Open **Explore → Radio communications** and press **Add to library** on a sheet (band
plans, licence-free channels, emergency frequencies). Open it from the library; searching
for a frequency, such as `446.00625`, finds its line.

## Repeaters

1. Open **Radio → Repeaters**. If it is empty, fetch a list with the button or follow the
   steps to download one and choose **Import a list**.
2. Choose a place and a band (2 m, 70 cm) to list repeaters by distance.
3. Press a repeater for the frequencies to listen and transmit on, its access, and **Show
   on the map**, **Check line of sight** or **Estimated range**.

A list is only as right as its date. Transmitting through a repeater needs an amateur
licence for the band.

## Send a message by mesh radio

Once a radio is set up under **Settings → Mesh radio**: open **Messages**, choose a radio
channel beside **Home**, press **Write**, and send. The count shows the bytes used (200 for
Meshtastic, 133 for MeshCore); a message over the limit is refused. Anyone in range can
read a mesh's default channel, and radio messages can be lost: this is not a way to call
the emergency services.
