# Radio quick start

For anyone in the household who did not set Outpost up. Everything here works with no
internet. Listening needs no licence; transmitting does, except on the licence-free
channels.

## Find something to listen to

1. Open **Radio → Frequencies to listen to**.
2. Narrow the list with the filters: what you want to hear (time signals, news and
   warnings, weather, distress, amateur, licence-free), your country, and **a set like
   mine** for the kind of radio in your hand (a kitchen FM and medium wave set, a
   shortwave portable, a scanner, a marine or CB set).
3. Read the frequency from the row, large, as your radio shows it: kHz below 30 MHz,
   MHz above. Tune to it.
4. Press the station's name for what you will hear, when it is on, and the mode to set
   the radio to.

To have it on paper, **Make a PDF** writes the list as it is filtered.

## The frequency sheets

Band plans, licence-free channels (PMR446, CB), and emergency and calling frequencies
are sheets that ship with Outpost.

1. Open **Explore** and the category **Radio communications**.
2. Press **Add to library** on a sheet. It is copied there and then, with no internet.
3. Open it from the library. Searching for a frequency, such as `446.00625`, finds the
   line it is on.

The sheets say what may be transmitted where. Outpost itself does not.

## Repeaters

Outpost holds no repeaters until someone brings a list in.

1. Open **Radio → Repeaters**.
2. If it is empty, the screen says where to get a list and how: press the button for a
   list that may be fetched, or follow the steps to download one by hand and choose
   **Import a list**.
3. Choose a place (home, the middle of the map, or where you are) to list repeaters by
   distance, and a band (2 m, 70 cm).
4. Press a repeater for the frequency to listen on and the one to transmit on, its
   access, and **Show on the map**, **Check line of sight** or **Estimated range**.

A list is only as right as its date, which every screen shows. Transmitting through a
repeater needs an amateur licence for the band.

## Send a message by mesh radio

Only when a Meshtastic or MeshCore radio has been set up under **Settings → Mesh radio**.
This has not yet been tried with a real radio.

1. Open **Messages**. Beside **Home** are the radio channels.
2. Choose a channel and press **Write**.
3. Type the message. The count under it says how many bytes of the radio's limit you
   have used (200 for Meshtastic, 133 for MeshCore). Nothing is cut: a message over the
   limit is refused, so shorten it.
4. Send. The message then says what became of it, such as **Handed to the radio**.

A mesh's default channel can be read by anyone with a radio in range: write nothing
private there. Radio messages can be lost or delayed; this is not a way to call the
emergency services.
