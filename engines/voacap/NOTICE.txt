The HF propagation engine in Outpost
====================================

Outpost's HF propagation planner does not contain a propagation model of its own. It
runs VOACAP, the Voice of America Coverage Analysis Program, through these parts:

1. hfcast (engine/voacap.wasm)
   A translation of VOACAP into Rust by Jonathan McSweet, Apache License 2.0.
   https://github.com/jonathanmcsweet/hfcast-engine at the commit named in
   engine/engine.json. Its licence and notice are in engine/LICENSE-hfcast.txt and
   engine/NOTICE-hfcast.txt. Outpost builds it unchanged except for wasm.patch, which
   lets a test harness that Outpost never calls compile where there are no symbolic
   links. Outpost's own bridge to it (wrapper/) is MIT, with Outpost.

3. voacapl
   The Unix build of VOACAP by J.A. Watson, whose changes to the original are released
   under CC0 1.0 Universal. https://github.com/jawatson/voacapl. Its two GPL programs
   (dst2csv, dst2ascii) are not used and not included.

4. The data files
   Two sets, and only the first ships with Outpost.

   Shipped (data/itshfbc/, listed with checksums in data/dataset.json): the antenna
   definition files of antennas/default/ (except the two named below) and
   database/version.w32, 29 files, 14,548 bytes. Part of the VOACAP distribution,
   NTIA/ITS work, under the notices in 2. They are copied unchanged from the hfcast
   repository's embedded/ folder. The files named ccir.000 to ccir.025 are VOACAP's own
   input definitions for antenna types that carry CCIR's names (HR 4/4/1 and the like):
   a type code, dimensions and two gain figures each; they are treated as NTIA/ITS work,
   as the hfcast project records them.

   Not shipped (listed in coefficients.json; fetched when a person asks, or at set-up):
   15 files, 559,336 bytes.

   - coeffs/coeff01w.bin to coeff12w.bin (38,552 bytes each). Each month's file holds
     the M(3000)F2 map of CCIR Report 340 and the atmospheric radio noise maps of CCIR
     Report 322, with NTIA/ITS's sporadic-E, E, F1 and F2 height-ratio maps and
     prediction-error tables in the same file. Mixed, so treated as CCIR-derived.
   - coeffs/fof2CCIR.daw (94,848 bytes): the foF2 maps of CCIR Report 340.
   - antennas/default/ccir.026 (921 bytes) and antennas/default/swwhip.voa (943 bytes):
     a short whip's elevation pattern, 91 figures, headed "SWWhip for REC533". Where the
     figures come from is not stated anywhere read, so they are treated as CCIR-derived.

   Which file holds what is the hfcast project's own reading of VOACAP's REDMAP, record
   by record (engine/NOTICE-hfcast.txt and its docs/licence.md): about 195 kB of NTIA/ITS
   tables, 216 kB of Report 322 noise and 134 kB of Report 340 maps, in the 13 files
   above. That reading was not checked against the CCIR Reports themselves.

   Why the second set is not shipped. The CCIR data was bundled on the strength of a
   statement in the reference software of ITU-R Study Group 3
   (https://github.com/ITU-R-Study-Group-3/ITU-R-HF, read on 16 November 2026):

     The ITURHFProp, P533 and P372 software has been developed collaboratively by
     participants in ITU-R Study Group 3. It may be used by implementers in their
     implementation of the Recommendation as well as in revisions of the specific
     original Recommendation and in other ITU Recommendations, free from any copyright
     assertions.

   Four header files in that same repository (P533/Src/P533/Common.h,
   P372/Src/P372/Common.h, P372/Src/ITURNoise/Common.h and
   ITURHFProp/Src/ITURHFProp/Common.h) say:

     Copyright International Telecommunication Union (ITU) 2018
     All rights reserved.
     No part of this publication may be reproduced, by any means whatsoever,
     without written permission of ITU

   The first is worded for implementations of ITU Recommendations, and VOACAP is not
   one; the second reserves all rights. Outpost does not bundle copyrighted material
   without permission, so these files are not in its repository or its packaged app.

   Where they are fetched from. The engine's own repository, at the commit the engine
   was built from:

     https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jonathanmcsweet/hfcast-engine/
       bf34e8f9f375e190ed43fcac19f25d800992e0e6/embedded/<file>

   On 16 November 2026 every one of the 15 files fetched from there was byte for byte
   the file this repository held until then. The project publishes them in a public
   repository; it offers them under no licence, and says so. Its NOTICE reads:

     Apache-2.0 covers the software in this repository. It does not cover these data
     files, which are the work of other bodies and keep their own status. They are
     listed here so that status travels with them.

   and its embedded/README.md:

     Whether these files may be redistributed to other people is an open question,
     recorded with its reasoning in `docs/licence.md`. Embedding them for builds used
     on one's own devices is not redistribution, which is why they are here while that
     stays undecided.

   NTIA/ITS publishes VOACAP as a Windows installer; voacapl publishes the
   coefficients only as text sources that its installer converts. Neither offers these
   exact files. Outpost fetches them for one person's own use, when that person asks,
   and does not pass them on. Each is checked against the size and SHA-256 checksum in
   coefficients.json, and the engine refuses to run on a set that is not whole or does
   not match.
